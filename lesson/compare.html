Locus fix: before / after

← Sound explorer

Experimental, not yet adopted. The original suli model's Acute locus (2500–2700Hz) overlaps the High vowel (2600Hz), and Grave (1200–1900Hz) overlaps the Low vowel (1750Hz) — so some consonants barely move in pitch at all next to certain vowels. si (Acute+High) in the original is completely flat: zero pitch movement, same as a Mid-locus consonant. This page compares the original against suli-wide, an experimental variant that widens Acute and Grave to clear the vowel range, following the same principle Annie Rialland measured in real Silbo Gomero (loci “frame” the vowel space from outside it). Cells flagged FLAT have no pitch movement at all in the original.
